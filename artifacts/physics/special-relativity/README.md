# Special Relativity

Postulates, Lorentz transformations, time dilation and length contraction, relativistic velocity addition, spacetime intervals, four-vectors, relativistic energy and momentum, electromagnetism in relativistic form (tensor formulation)

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.
