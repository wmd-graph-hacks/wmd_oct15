# Classical Mechanics

Newton’s laws, work-energy theorem, conservation of momentum and energy, angular momentum, central force motion, Kepler problem, rigid body dynamics (inertia tensor, Euler equations), oscillations (damped, driven, resonance), small oscillations and normal modes, Lagrangian mechanics, Hamiltonian mechanics, Poisson brackets, introduction to chaos

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.
