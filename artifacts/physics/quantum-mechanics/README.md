# Quantum Mechanics

Wave-particle duality, Schrödinger equation (time-dependent and time-independent), wave functions and probability interpretation, operators and observables, commutators, uncertainty principle, particle in a box, harmonic oscillator (ladder operators), finite potential wells, tunneling, hydrogen atom, angular momentum and spin, addition of angular momenta, perturbation theory (time-independent and time-dependent), variational method, identical particles and the Pauli exclusion principle, density matrix (intro)

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.
