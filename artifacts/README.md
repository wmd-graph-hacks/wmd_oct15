## Suggested cross-subject route

1. Start with mathematics and the relevant science or computing foundations.
2. Study physics, biology, and chemistry alongside their mathematical and experimental methods.
3. Build computer science foundations before backend engineering and DevOps.
4. Study AI/ML after programming, statistics, and linear algebra foundations.
5. Study cybersecurity alongside systems and networking; all practical work must be authorized and scoped.

This is a broad learning map rather than a fixed timetable. Select topics based on learner goals and prerequisites.
