# Optimization

Choose best feasible decisions under linear or nonlinear objectives and constraints.

## Learning outcomes

- Explain and apply the core ideas in **Convex sets and functions**.
- Explain and apply the core ideas in **Linear programming, simplex, duality**.
- Explain and apply the core ideas in **KKT and constrained optimization**.
- Explain and apply the core ideas in **Gradient descent and variants**.

## Assessment evidence

A learner should be able to solve representative problems without hints, explain why the method applies, identify assumptions, and check the result. Suggested evidence: a short diagnostic, a worked solution with reasoning, and a spaced-retrieval check after several days.
