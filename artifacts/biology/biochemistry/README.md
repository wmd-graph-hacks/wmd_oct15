# Biochemistry

Amino acids and protein structure (primary through quaternary), enzyme kinetics (Michaelis-Menten, inhibition, allosteric regulation), carbohydrate metabolism (glycolysis, gluconeogenesis, glycogen metabolism, pentose phosphate pathway), citric acid cycle, oxidative phosphorylation and the electron transport chain, lipid metabolism (beta-oxidation, fatty acid synthesis), amino acid metabolism, nucleotide metabolism, photosynthesis (light reactions, Calvin cycle), bioenergetics and thermodynamics of living systems

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.
