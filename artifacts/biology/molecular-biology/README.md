# Molecular Biology

Structure of DNA and RNA, DNA replication (enzymes and mechanism), transcription (RNA polymerase, promoters, splicing), translation (ribosomes, tRNA, genetic code), post-translational modification, gene regulation (operons, transcription factors, enhancers, silencers), epigenetics (DNA methylation, histone modification, chromatin remodeling), DNA repair pathways, mutations and their consequences, mobile genetic elements, non-coding RNAs (miRNA, siRNA, lncRNA)

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.
