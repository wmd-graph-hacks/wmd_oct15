# Laboratory Skills and Safety

Good laboratory practice, glassware and instrumentation, safe handling of hazardous chemicals, waste disposal, data recording, error analysis, interpretation of experimental results

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.

Ask the learner to explain one concept without notes, work a changed example, and identify an assumption or limitation.
