# Physical Chemistry

Thermodynamics of mixtures, chemical potential, phase equilibria, quantum chemistry (Schrödinger equation for atoms and molecules, Hückel theory), statistical thermodynamics, spectroscopy (rotational, vibrational, electronic), surface chemistry and adsorption isotherms, photochemistry

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.

- [statistical thermodynamics: mechanisms and methods](./statistical-thermodynamics/README.md) (`chemistry-physical-chemistry-statistical-thermodynamics`)

Ask the learner to explain one concept without notes, work a changed example, and identify an assumption or limitation.
