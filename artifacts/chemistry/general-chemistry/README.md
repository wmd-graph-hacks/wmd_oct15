# General Chemistry

Atomic structure, electron configuration, periodic trends (atomic radius, ionization energy, electronegativity), stoichiometry, chemical reactions and balancing, gas laws, solutions and concentration units, colligative properties, states of matter, phase diagrams

## Learning outcomes

- Define the main terms and identify assumptions.
- Explain how the central ideas connect to each other and the wider subject.
- Apply a standard method or interpret an example in context.
- Check a conclusion and describe a limitation.
